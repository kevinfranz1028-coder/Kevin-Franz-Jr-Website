## Earning college credit now

Alongside Shawnee Mission West, I am a **full-time student at Johnson County Community College (JCCC)**. It runs through SMSD's **Real World Learning** program, which lets me take a real college course load and earn credit while I finish high school.

This fall I am taking **four JCCC classes**.

## Spring 2027

For the spring term I am registered for **15 credit hours**:

- College Algebra
- Composition II
- Mass Media and Society
- Personal Finance
- Public Speaking

A full 15-hour schedule on top of basketball means the time management has to be real. The habits I built balancing school and a season — using travel time, blocking out hours for coursework, asking for help early — are the only reason this is workable.

## Why it matters

Carrying a college course load now means arriving on a campus already having done the work, not just talked about it. It is the same reason I keep my [GPA](/academics/) where it is: I want the classroom to be a reason to recruit me, not a question mark.
