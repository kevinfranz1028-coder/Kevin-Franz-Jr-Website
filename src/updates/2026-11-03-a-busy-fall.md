## Two events in Kansas City

It has been a busy fall. In early October I competed at the **High School All-American (HSAA) Showcase** in Kansas City, and on **October 11** I played at **Prep Hoops' The Expo: Kansas City**, where I wore jersey **#927**.

Both events put you in front of a lot of people at once, against players you have never seen before. That is a different test from league play — you get a handful of possessions to show what you do well, and you have to be decisive.

## JC Hoops 16U

With **JC Hoops 16U** the team has gone **10-3** this fall. My line across those games:

- **8.2** points per game
- **5.5** rebounds per game
- **2.8** assists per game
- **38%** from three

Shooting 38% from deep is the number I am happiest with. Last winter with the BVSW JV I shot 32% from three.

## Between games

Off the court I have kept up consistent offseason workouts — the unglamorous part that makes the rest of it possible.
