## Where the semester stands

I am four classes into my first full-time semester at Johnson County Community College, taken through SMSD's Real World Learning program while I am still a high school student at Shawnee Mission West.

Here is where the four stand as of **November 10, 2026**, straight out of Canvas:

- **ENGL 121** Composition I — **92.79%**
- **MUS 128** — **95.52%**
- **GEOS 160** Meteorology — **88.31%**
- **ECON 230** — **82.93%**

**No missing work** in any of the four. These are in-progress numbers, not final grades — the semester is not over yet.

## The one I care most about

GEOS 160 is Meteorology, and it is the first time I have taken an actual college course in the thing I want to study. Reading about supercells on my own is one thing; being graded on the physics behind them is another. It is my lowest of the four at the moment and still the one I look forward to every week.

Composition I has been the most useful in a way I did not expect. Writing clearly under a deadline is a skill that carries into everything else — scouting reports, emails to coaches, all of it.

## Spring 2027

I am registered for **15 credit hours** at JCCC for the spring term, still alongside Shawnee Mission West:

- MATH 171 — College Algebra
- ENGL 122 — Composition II
- JOUR 120 — Mass Media & Society
- BUS 123 — Personal Finance
- COMS 121 — Public Speaking

Fifteen hours on top of a high school schedule and a basketball season means the time management has to be real. The habits I built balancing school and a season — using travel time, blocking out hours for coursework, asking for help early — are the only reason this is workable.
