## Freshman year at BVSW, #44

My first year of high school basketball. I was a **captain of the freshman team** and started 14 of 16 games.

**Season line:** 5.3 PPG · 3.1 RPG · 2.0 APG · **2.1 SPG** · 0.7 BPG · 39% FG · 50% FT

The team record was 4-12, so it was a losing season, but the 2.1 steals a game held up against high school speed. Defending full court was the part of my game that translated fastest.

## Spring club — JC Hoops 15U, #28

- **20 games, 20 starts:** 5.3 PPG · 2.9 RPG · 2.0 APG · 1.3 SPG, team record 12-8

Starting all 20 games as a 15U was the first time I was relied on every night. My shooting percentages that spring were poor (27% FG), and fixing that became the focus of the next year of work.

## What I took from it

Being a captain as a freshman on a team that lost a lot taught me more about leadership than a winning season would have. Keeping teammates engaged when the record is 4-12 is genuinely hard.
