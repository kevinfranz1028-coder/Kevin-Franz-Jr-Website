## Winter 2025-26

Sophomore year on the Blue Valley Southwest JV squad, wearing #30. The team finished 7-14 across 21 games, and I appeared in 19 of them with 17 starts.

- Season-high 14 points against Bishop Miege on 5-of-7 shooting

- 48 assists against 22 turnovers across the season

The assist-to-turnover number is the one I am proudest of. Taking care of the ball is the part of my game I trust most.
